# Printer

The Printer settings page lets you configure your receipt printer for use with the POS system.

---

## Configuration

Set up your printer with the following options:

- **Printer ID**: The unique identifier for the printer.
- **Header**: Custom text that appears at the top of every receipt (e.g., your store name, address, phone number).
- **Footer**: Custom text that appears at the bottom of every receipt.
- **Name**: The name of the printer device.
- **Driver**: Select the connection type — **USB** is the primary supported option.
- **Printer**: Configure the printer port/device.

---

## Tips

- Test your printer after configuration by running a sample receipt from the POS.
- Keep your header concise — it appears on every receipt and label.
- If the printer isn't responding, verify the driver selection and printer match your hardware setup.
