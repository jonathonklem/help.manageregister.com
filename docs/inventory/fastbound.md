# FastBound

The FastBound integration connects your inventory system with FastBound's electronic A&D (Acquisition & Disposition) book for firearms compliance tracking.

---

## What FastBound Does

FastBound is used to maintain an electronic bound book as required by the ATF. The integration allows you to:

- Review firearms acquisitions and dispositions
- Keep compliance records in sync with your inventory
- Access FastBound records directly from within ManageRegister

---

## Reviewing Records

Go to **Inventory > FastBound** to access the FastBound review interface. From here you can review and manage your electronic bound book entries.

---

## Tips

- Make sure your FastBound account is properly configured and linked before using this feature.
- Regularly review your FastBound records to ensure compliance with ATF requirements.
- Contact your FastBound account representative if you have questions about compliance.
