# Z-Report

The Z-Report is your end-of-day cash register reconciliation report. It summarizes all sales activity for a register during a specific period and helps you verify that your cash drawer matches expected totals.

---

## What the Z-Report Shows

- **Starting Cash**: The amount in the drawer at the start of the shift.
- **Sales Summary**: Total revenue broken down by payment type.
- **Payment Method Breakdown**: How much was collected via cash, credit, debit, etc.
- **Discrepancy**: The difference between expected and actual cash totals.

---

## Running a Z-Report

1. Go to **Report > Z-Report**.
2. Select the **Report Mode**:
   - **Daily (Calendar Day)**: Aggregates all transactions for a full calendar day (midnight to midnight in your timezone). Pick a date and register.
   - **Custom Range (Shift)**: Enter a custom start and end time for a specific shift period.
3. Select the **Cash Register** you want to report on.
4. Optionally enter **Starting Cash** and **Counted Cash** to calculate variance. You can use the **Change Counter** (see below) to calculate your counted cash total from individual denominations.
5. Click **Generate Z-Report**.

### Daily Mode

Daily mode shows all transactions for the selected date, with shift-by-shift breakdowns as sub-sections if multiple cash sessions occurred during the day. The totals reflect the full day.

### Shift Mode

Shift mode shows only the transactions within your custom time range. Use this for mid-day shift changes or partial-day reporting.

---

## Change Counter

The Change Counter helps you count the cash in your drawer by denomination instead of manually adding it up.

1. Click **Count Change** (below the report form).
2. Enter the number of each bill and coin type in your drawer:
   - **Bills**: $100, $50, $20, $10, $5, $1
   - **Coins**: Quarters (25¢), Dimes (10¢), Nickels (5¢), Pennies (1¢)
3. The **Total** updates automatically as you enter counts.
4. Click **Apply to Counted Cash** to populate the Counted Cash field in the form above.
5. Generate your Z-Report — the variance calculation will use this total.

Use the **Clear** button to reset all denomination counts to zero.

---

## Report History

Previous Z-Reports are saved and can be viewed at any time. Use this to:

- Compare day-over-day performance.
- Investigate discrepancies after the fact.
- Provide documentation for bookkeeping.

---

## Tips

- Run a Z-Report at the end of every shift or business day.
- If there's a discrepancy, check for unreported petty cash withdrawals or missed transactions.
- Make sure all cashiers are using the correct register to avoid cross-register confusion.
