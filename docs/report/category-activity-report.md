# Category Activity Report

The Category Activity Report shows a month-by-month breakdown of how many units of each product sold within one or more categories. Each row is a product, each column is a month, and each cell is the quantity sold — with totals per month, per product, and a daily average.

It's ideal for tracking activity-style categories over time, such as range fees, rentals, classes, or memberships — anywhere you want to see "how many of these did we do each month?"

---

## Running the Report

1. Go to **Report > Category Activity Report**.
2. Select one or more **Categories** to include.
3. Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date** (defaults to year-to-date).
4. Optionally enable **Member / Non-member Split** (see below).
5. Click **Generate**.

---

## What You'll See

- One row per product in the selected categories
- One column per month in the selected date range
- **Total** — total quantity sold per product across the whole period
- **Avg/Day** — the product's total divided by the number of days in the period
- A **Total** row at the bottom summing each month and the grand total

Products that have been deleted but sold during the period are still included, so historical totals stay accurate.

---

## Member / Non-member Split

Turn on the **Member Split** toggle to break each cell into member vs non-member quantities. A sale counts as a member sale when a member is attached to the transaction at checkout.

- On screen, each cell shows the total with a smaller `member / non-member` line underneath.
- In the CSV export, each product gets three rows: **Total**, **Member**, and **Non-member**.

---

## Printing and Exporting

- **Print** — prints just the report table, formatted for paper.
- **Download CSV** — exports the full matrix (including the split rows if enabled) for use in Excel or Google Sheets.

---

## Tips

- For a gun range: put your lane fees, guest fees, and rental items in a dedicated category, then run this report to replicate a yearly range-usage tally in one click.
- Compare the same months across different years by running the report per year and exporting to CSV.
- Use **Avg/Day** to compare products fairly across periods of different lengths.
