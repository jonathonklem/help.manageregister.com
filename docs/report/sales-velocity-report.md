# Sales Velocity Report

The Sales Velocity Report shows how quickly products are selling over a given time period. Use it to identify your best and worst performers and make informed purchasing decisions.

---

## Running the Report

1. Go to **Report > Sales Velocity Report**.
2. Select the **date range** to analyze.
3. Review the results.

---

## What You'll See

- Products ranked by sales volume or revenue
- Sales trends over the selected period
- Performance comparisons across products

---

## Tips

- Use this report to identify slow-moving inventory that may need markdowns or promotions.
- Compare velocity across different time periods to spot seasonal trends.
