# Sales Tax Report

The Sales Tax Report provides a summary of all taxes collected during a selected time period. Use it to prepare for tax filings and verify that the correct tax amounts are being applied to sales.

---

## Running the Report

1. Go to **Report > Sales Tax Report**.
2. Select the **date range** you want to report on.
3. Review the tax summary.

---

## What You'll See

- Total taxable sales
- Total tax collected
- Breakdown by tax rate (if applicable)

---

## Tips

- Run this report at the end of each month or quarter to stay on top of tax obligations.
- If tax amounts look incorrect, verify your tax rate under **Setting > General Setting**.
