# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after logging in. It provides a quick overview of your store's sales activity.

---

## 📊 What You'll See

- **Selling Overview**: A summary of recent sales performance including total revenue, number of transactions, and trends.
- **Today's Best Selling Products**: A quick look at which items are moving the most today.

---

## 🧠 Tips

- Use the Dashboard to spot trends at a glance before diving into detailed reports.
- If numbers look off, check that your registers are active and transactions are being recorded properly under **Setting > Cash Register**.
